VOLUNTEER PLAYBOOK

goSTOPS Rishikesh, Lakshman Jhula — no assumed prerequisites, no extra equipment. Everything below runs on what's already in the hostel.

🎯 PROMOTING PROPERTY BOOKINGS & goLOCAL EXPERIENCES

goSTOPS runs its own version of tour packages under goLOCAL Experiences (part of the goEXPERIENCES program) — third-party-vetted activities like the Kunjapuri sunrise trek, Vashisht Cave tour, rafting, and food/heritage walks, bookable through the property or the goSTOPS app's community space, Caravan. As a volunteer, the pitch isn't "sell rooms" — it's making guests who are already here book the next thing and tell their friends. A few ways in:

  • Common-area concierge hour: a low-key evening slot where you sit in the common area with the goLOCAL board/menu and talk guests through it over chai — peer recommendation converts far better than a poster on the wall.
  • Group-up whiteboard: a hand-written "who's doing what tomorrow" board near reception so solo travelers self-organize into the same sunrise-trek or rafting slot — bigger groups make the upsell easier and cost the hostel nothing.
  • Guest reel capture: a 10-second phone-camera testimonial right after check-in or after a goLOCAL activity, edited same evening and handed to the property's social/marketing volunteer or posted to Caravan — real footage beats stock photos.
  • Farewell story wall: handwritten notes from checking-out guests about their favourite experience, pinned near reception — social proof for the next arrival, and a nice keepsake wall for the property itself.

🎲 DAILY ENTERTAINMENT ROSTER — MIDDAY & EVENING (TILL 10 PM)

2-3 options per slot per day, so there's always a backup if the first idea doesn't land. Built for a mixed room — solo backpackers, families, and the more well-read/well-off crowd alike — using only the common area, rooftop, yoga space, games room, and whatever's already lying around.

VOLUNTEER DAY 1 — THU 30 JUL

  • Midday: "Desk Job Roulette" — guests write one odd skill on a chit, shuffle, redistribute, and guess who wrote what over lunch. Backup: an impromptu rooftop tea-tasting flight using the café's existing chai/coffee menu, with you narrating a story per cup.
  • Evening: Piggyback on the hostel's existing movie/sports night with a 10-minute "guess the film from 3 emojis" warm-up round. Backup: a self-seeded billiards/pool-table knockout ladder on the whiteboard — scales from 4 to 40 people.

VOLUNTEER DAY 2 — FRI 31 JUL

  • Midday: "Postcard You'll Never Send" — spare paper, guests write to their past/future self, a few read anonymously later. Backup: a 10-minute guided breathing/stretch session in the existing yoga space, no instructor required.
  • Evening: goLOCAL pitch session over chai (ties directly into the promotion ideas above). Backup: board-game speed-swap — tables rotate games from the existing games-room stock every 15 minutes.

VOLUNTEER DAY 3 — SAT 01 AUG

  • Midday: A 20-minute Ganga ghat walk with one real question per person ("best decision you made this year") to spark conversation among strangers. Backup: common-area trivia — Rishikesh/Himalaya/Beatles Ashram facts, written up in the Classmate notebook, zero internet needed.
  • Evening: Live-jamming night (already scheduled) with you MC-ing a 2-song "audience requests only" segment to pull in non-musicians. Backup: a Silent Reading Party — everyone reads whatever they've got (or borrows from the hostel shelf) for 30 minutes, then a 10-minute share-out — a hit with the quieter, well-read crowd.

VOLUNTEER DAY 4 — SUN 02 AUG

  • Midday: A "watch me get this wrong" cook-along with the café staff (chai or a simple snack) that guests can join. Backup: a photo scavenger hunt confined entirely to hostel property — rooftop, courtyard, signage — so it works for every fitness level.
  • Evening: Sports night (existing) plus a low-stakes "hostel Olympics" round — arm wrestling, paper-plane distance throw, one-leg balance. Backup: "Debate Club Lite" — a harmless topic like "is a hot dog a sandwich", two random guests argue it for 3 minutes each — scales well for the sharper, more argumentative crowd without alienating anyone else.

VOLUNTEER DAY 5 — MON 03 AUG

  • Midday: An informal sun-salutation/stretch session in the yoga space, framed as a "pre-lunch reset." Backup: a quiet rooftop journaling hour, using your own notebook as the visual prompt — good for solo travelers who'd rather not join a group activity.
  • Evening: Movie night (existing) with a "predict the ending" side bet on paper before the film starts. Backup: an "Ask a Local(ish)" open Q&A where you and any long-stay guests field newcomers' questions about Rishikesh — genuinely useful and good goodwill for the property.

VOLUNTEER DAY 6 — TUE 04 AUG (WRAP-UP)

  • Midday: A "pack-and-swap" table where departing guests leave unwanted-but-useful items (chargers, snacks, guidebooks) for others. Backup: the rooftop farewell photo/note wall from the promotion section above.
  • Evening: Lights-dimmed "campfire stories" in the common area — no fire needed, just chairs and one real travel story per person. Backup: an acoustic sing-along using whatever guests can hum or remember the words to — no mic or backing track required.
